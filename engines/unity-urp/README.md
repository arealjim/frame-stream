# unity-urp: a larger Unity environment on the Frame stream

A URP stress scene for the Frame stream. It is a Unity project on the Mac at `~/src/unity-urp`, built from the
editor's bundled *Universal 3D* template, filled with a generated environment, and configured for OXRSys the same
way as `../unity`. The scripts live in `../unity`; this directory holds only these notes.

**Status:** it plays, streams and has been worn. With the baked settings (Single Pass Instanced, URP MSAA 2x, one
shadow cascade at 25 m, point/spot shadows off, post-processing off) it runs at about 90 Hz worn over USB, eyes at
2160x2160.

## The scene

The editor's two bundled templates (`Unity.app/Contents/Resources/PackageManager/ProjectTemplates/`) are near-empty:

| template | tgz | scene | objects |
|---|---|---|---|
| `com.unity.template.3d-cross-platform-17.0.14` (Universal 3D, URP) | 210 KB | `Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity` | 17 (camera, directional light, Global Volume) |
| `com.unity.template.3d-high-end-17.0.7` (High-End 3D, HDRP) | 210 KB | `Assets/OutdoorsScene.unity` | 20 |

Unity's real sample environments (the URP construction site, Boat Attack, the HDRP sample) come from the Asset Store
or GitHub, and an Asset Store download needs a signed-in person. So the environment is generated by
`Editor/FrameLabStress.cs` (in `../unity`) on top of the URP template's scene. It keeps the template's Global
Volume, directional light and skybox, and adds:

- a 200 m Perlin terrain (257x257 heightmap, 30 m of relief, flat within 6 m of the camera) with a 1024² procedural
  ground texture, URP `Terrain/Lit`
- **1944 renderers**: a 36x36 field of cubes, spheres, cylinders and capsules on a 2 m pitch (0.15-0.9 m), 300
  two-part "trees" out to 90 m, 40 slabs, and a 16-piece near ring at 1.6 m (thin edges, to judge shimmer), in 24
  URP/Lit materials (6 textured, 3 transparent, 4 metallic)
- 12 point lights (2 with soft shadows), 1 shadowed spot and the template's directional light; a 256² realtime
  reflection probe
- the template's volume: Tonemapping, Bloom and Vignette on; Motion Blur present but off. The PC renderer carries
  the ScreenSpaceAmbientOcclusion feature.

URP asset as generated: `PC_RPAsset` (quality level 1 = PC, the Mac's), HDR on, camera depth and opaque textures on
(a copy per eye in XR), main shadowmap 2048, shadow distance 50 m, 4 cascades, additional lights per-pixel (max 4
per object), render scale 1, upscaling Auto, MSAA 4x. `Budget90` (below) changes several of these.

Scene file `Assets/Scenes/Stress.unity`: 4.9 MB, 7845 YAML objects. Colour space Linear, input handling Both. The
generator also writes `Assets/Stress/` (58 assets: textures, materials, terrain data, layer).

## Build (on the Mac, editor 6000.3.26f1)

    U=~/Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.3.26f1/Unity.app/Contents/MacOS/Unity
    T=~/Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.3.26f1/Unity.app/Contents/Resources/PackageManager/ProjectTemplates/com.unity.template.3d-cross-platform-17.0.14.tgz
    P=~/src/unity-urp
    nice -n 15 "$U" -batchmode -createProject $P -cloneFromTemplate "$T" -quit -logFile ~/Library/Logs/frame-stream/unity-urp-create.log
    rsync -a <repo>/engines/unity/Editor/ $P/Assets/Editor/
    rsync -a <repo>/engines/unity/Scripts/ $P/Assets/Scripts/
    nice -n 15 "$U" -batchmode -projectPath $P -executeMethod FrameLab.Bootstrap.AddPackages -quit -logFile ~/Library/Logs/frame-stream/unity-urp-stage1.log
    nice -n 15 "$U" -batchmode -projectPath $P -executeMethod FrameLab.Stress.Build        -quit -logFile ~/Library/Logs/frame-stream/unity-urp-stress.log
    nice -n 15 "$U" -batchmode -projectPath $P -executeMethod FrameLab.Stress.UpgradeMaterials -quit -logFile ~/Library/Logs/frame-stream/unity-urp-upgrade.log   # else Play hangs on a URP dialog
    FRAMELAB_SCENE=Assets/Scenes/Stress.unity \
    nice -n 15 "$U" -batchmode -projectPath $P -executeMethod FrameLab.Bootstrap.Configure -quit -logFile ~/Library/Logs/frame-stream/unity-urp-stage2.log
    nice -n 15 "$U" -batchmode -projectPath $P -executeMethod FrameLab.Stress.Budget90 -quit -logFile ~/Library/Logs/frame-stream/unity-urp-budget90.log   # after Configure (it sets MultiPass): the 90 Hz budget (section "90 Hz")

What each step does and what it logs:

| step | result |
|---|---|
| create | the template's Assets, Packages and ProjectSettings. Exits 132 (see the gotcha on exit codes) |
| `Bootstrap.AddPackages` | com.unity.xr.openxr 1.18.0, xr.management 4.6.1, xr.core-utils 2.6.0, legacyinputhelpers 2.1.13, inputsystem 1.20.1. URP resolves up from the template's 17.0.1 to **17.3.0** |
| `Stress.Build` | "1944 renderers + terrain 257x257, 13 extra lights, volume [Tonemapping, Bloom, MotionBlur(off), Vignette], camera at (0.0, 1.0, -10.0) clear Skybox; colour space Linear". `FRAMELAB_SCENE` picks the input scene (default `SampleScene`); `FRAMELAB_MSAA` sets the URP asset's MSAA |
| `Stress.UpgradeMaterials` | runs URP's own `ReimportAllMaterials` and `SaveAssetsToDisk` by reflection, with no dialog. Logs "URP material version before 9" and "after 10" |
| `Bootstrap.Configure` | OpenXR loader assigned, HandInteractionProfile + MetaQuestTouchPlusControllerProfile, render mode MultiPass, depth submission None. With `FRAMELAB_SCENE` set it adopts that scene (below) |
| `Stress.Budget90` | the settings that reach 90 Hz (section "90 Hz") |

**Adopting a scene.** With `FRAMELAB_SCENE=<Assets/...unity>`, Configure adopts that scene instead of building the
smoke one. It finds `Camera.main` (else the first Camera), sets near clip 0.05, and parents it under a new
`XR Origin (floor)` at the camera's x/z on the floor, facing the camera's yaw. The origin's y is 0 when the camera
sits 0.5-3 m up, else the camera's y minus 1.6. It adds `TrackedPoseDriver` and `FrameLabRig`, saves, and makes
the scene the only build scene. For Stress.unity the log reads: "camera 'Main Camera' was at (0.00, 1.00, -10.00)
yaw 0 -> origin at (0.00, 0.00, -10.00); renderers 1944 lights 14 clear Skybox cube absent". The rig's cube and props
appear only when the scene has one; otherwise the rig logs `cube missing`, which is expected here.

`Play` and `BuildPlayer` open `FRAMELAB_SCENE`, else `Assets/Scenes/Smoke.unity` if it exists, else the build
list's first scene. So Play in unity-urp opens Stress.unity with no environment variable.

`Editor/FrameLab.Editor.asmdef` references `Unity.RenderPipelines.Universal.Runtime` and
`Unity.RenderPipelines.Core.Runtime`. `FrameLabStress.cs` is wrapped in `#if FRAMELAB_URP`, a version define on
`com.unity.render-pipelines.universal`, so the same Editor folder still compiles in the built-in-pipeline smoke
project.

## Run

    FRAME_STREAM_UNITY_PROJECT=$HOME/src/unity-urp ./bin/frame-stream up --app unity --mac-ip <mac-ip>

frame-stream waits for the rig's `frame-lab rig: scene 'Stress' ...` line. Any `FRAMELAB_*` variable in its
environment reaches the editor, so the A/B knobs below work through it.

Regression run with the client attached:

    FRAME_STREAM_UNITY_PROJECT=$HOME/src/unity-urp FRAME_STREAM_MAC_IP=<mac-ip> REGRESS_SECONDS=90 tools/regress.sh unity

A healthy play log (`~/Library/Logs/frame-stream/unity-play.log`) shows `opened Assets/Scenes/Stress.unity`, then the rig line with
`scene 'Stress'`, `clear Skybox`, `floor True`, `cube missing`, `xr OpenXR Display 2160x2160`, `tracking 3`, then
`Stereo Rendering Mode: SinglePassInstanced` (after Budget90) and `Metal swapchain created 2160x2160` twice. The first
`WaitFrame pacing` line arrives about 10 s after the editor launch; the first frame takes about 0.9 s.

From the floor origin at (0, 0, -10): the near ring sits 1.0-1.8 m around the eye, the object field starts beyond
3 m, trees and slabs reach 90 m, and the shadowed spot is overhead.

### Knobs

- `FRAMELAB_STEPS="<s>:<key>=<value>;..."` (`Scripts/FrameLabBudget.cs`) applies settings at play, `<s>` seconds in.
  Keys: `msaa`, `scale`, `shadowdist`, `lightshadows` (point/spot), `postfx`, `eyescale`, `shadowres`, `cascades`,
  `queued` (maxQueuedFrames). It logs the current settings and a timing line every 450 frames: frame interval,
  GPU time, main thread, render thread and present wait.
- `FRAMELAB_RENDERMODE=SinglePassInstanced|MultiPass` makes `Bootstrap.Play` write the OpenXR render mode.
- `FRAMELAB_MSAA` on the `Stress.Build` run sets MSAA in the URP asset. The rig's `FRAMELAB_MSAA` at play sets
  `QualitySettings.antiAliasing`, which URP ignores. Use `FRAMELAB_STEPS="0:msaa=..."` at play instead.

## Gotchas

- **Play hangs on a hidden "URP Material upgrade" dialog unless `Stress.UpgradeMaterials` has run.** The template's
  materials are at URP material version 9 (`ProjectSettings/URPProjectSettings.asset`, `m_LastMaterialVersion`); URP
  17.3 has 10 upgraders. The check runs on the first rendered editor frame, so batch mode never shows it and Play
  always does. The editor then sits at about 0.3 % CPU with no `WaitFrame pacing` line. The runtime sees the session
  reach "Attached 2 action sets" and nothing after, and the client says "no server found", because the server only
  answers discovery once frames flow. Fix: run `FrameLab.Stress.UpgradeMaterials` after `Stress.Build` on a fresh
  project. To identify a stuck dialog: `sample <pid>` shows the main thread in
  `EditorDialog_CUSTOM_DisplayAlertDialogNative`.
- **A `-quit` run's exit code is not a verdict.** The editor often dies in its own shutdown (exit 132, SIGILL) after
  "Batchmode quit successfully invoked" and "Thread 0x... may have been prematurely finalized". The work is done by
  then. Read the log for `error CS` instead.
- **URP ignores `QualitySettings.antiAliasing`.** The pipeline asset owns MSAA. Set it with `FRAMELAB_STEPS`
  (`msaa`), `FRAMELAB_MSAA` on the generator run, or `m_MSAA` in `Assets/Settings/PC_RPAsset.asset`.
- **Render scale below 1 breaks the stream.** URP drives `XRSettings.eyeTextureResolutionScale` from it, so the runtime
  makes a smaller swapchain (`Metal swapchain created 1944x1944` at 0.9). Foveated encoding refuses an eye buffer below
  the 2160 target. The runtime's res_scale does not reduce what Unity renders either.
- **Shader warnings on the first import of a fresh project are harmless.** 18 `Shader Unsupported: ... All subshaders
  removed` triplets for `Terrain/Lit`, its Base Pass, `VR/SpatialMapping/Wireframe`, plus `fallback shader ... not
  found` lines. They are import order: each shader was imported before the hidden shader it depends on. Later runs
  carry none, and play logs no shader, Terrain/Lit, SSAO, XR or Metal error.
- **Other harmless log lines:** an `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` in `UnityEditor.Search.SearchDatabase` (the editor's
  QuickSearch indexer, every launch), `[Licensing::Module] Error: Access token is unavailable`, and a `Curl error 60`
  pair.
- Configure leaves a second `Assets/XR/Settings 1/` beside `Settings/` (XR Management creates its default assets
  twice across the two stages). Harmless; `XRGeneralSettingsPerBuildTarget.asset` is the registered one.

## 90 Hz

`FrameLab.Stress.Budget90` bakes the settings that hold 90 Hz with eyes still at 2160x2160:

- OpenXR render mode **Single Pass Instanced**
- URP **MSAA 2x**
- **shadows off** on the point and spot lights
- **one shadow cascade, shadow distance 25 m**
- **post-processing off** on the camera

With it, a plain Play logs `Stereo Rendering Mode: SinglePassInstanced`, `msaa 2 ... postfx False`.

### As generated, it does not hold 90 Hz

MultiPass, MSAA 4x, all shadows and post on, Mac only (no client), 45 s: WaitFrame mean **14.25 ms** (13.75-14.61),
about 71 of 90 frames late, so a steady ~70 Hz. `sudo powermetrics --samplers gpu_power,cpu_power -i 2000 -n 3`
during that run: GPU 86.7 % active at its top step (1600 MHz), 26.9 W; CPU 5.3 W, Unity 62 % of one core. The smoke
scene at the same resolution paces 11.11 ms with 0 late.

With the client attached over the tailnet (headset unworn, `regress.sh unity`, 90 s): render 16.0 ms, enc+send 11.7,
display age 10.5, rtt 5.3, 187 of 270 late, 62 fresh frames/s of 90, no loss, no encoder drops.

### Mac only: one knob at a time

Each row is its own launch with `FRAMELAB_STEPS` set and no client, steady state 40-47 s in. Budget is 11.11 ms.

| render mode | MSAA | point/spot shadows | other | WaitFrame mean (ms) | app late /90 | Unity GPU (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MultiPass | 4x | on | | 14.25 | ~70 Hz | 9.98 |
| SPI | 4x | on | | 13.80 | 73 | 11.09 |
| SPI | 2x | on | | 12.77 | 79 | 10.09 |
| SPI | 4x | off | | 12.15 | 83 | 9.61 |
| MultiPass | 2x | off | | 12.18 | 83 | 8.12 |
| SPI | 2x | off | | 11.26 | 89 | 8.74 |
| SPI | 2x | off | shadow distance 50 -> 25 m | 11.11 | 2-6 | 8.68 |
| **SPI** | **2x** | **off** | **post-processing off** | **11.11** | **0** | 8.06 |
| SPI | off | off | | 11.11 | 0 | 7.82 |
| SPI | 2x | off | render scale 0.9 | 11.11 | 0 | 7.09, swapchain 1944x1944, rejected |

Unity's GPU time (8-9 ms) sits well under the 11-14 ms frame, so SPI's gain is mostly CPU and submission. That is
why both SPI and the shadow cut count.

### MSAA 2x to 90

With the client attached, the Mac-only winner (SPI, MSAA 2x, point/spot shadows off, post off, shadow distance 50,
4 cascades) does not hold 90 Hz. Tailnet, headset unworn: 75 fresh frames/s, 249 late, render 11.9 ms, encode+send
11.5 ms, display age 10.8 ms, rtt 5.3 ms, 0 lost, 0 encoder drops. Timing lines: main thread 1.4 ms, render thread
11.5 ms, present wait 10.2 ms, Unity GPU 8.7 ms. The main thread idles; the render thread, which carries the frame
submit into the in-process runtime, fills the frame.

MSAA 2x looks better than MSAA off when worn, so the shadow settings were cut instead. USB, headset unworn, client
attached, one launch per row, all MSAA 2x; numbers are FrameLabBudget 5 s windows:

| config | frame interval | Unity GPU (ms) |
|---|---|---|
| shadow distance 50, 4 cascades, 2048 | 12.2-12.4 ms (~81/s) | 8.68 |
| shadow distance 25 / 15 | 11.1-11.9 / 11.1-11.5 | 8.53 / 8.49 |
| no main-light shadows | 11.11 steady, 0 late | 8.20 |
| maxQueuedFrames 1 / 3 (default) / 4 | 12.2-12.4, unchanged | 8.68 |
| 2 cascades | 12.3-12.5 | 8.60 |
| 1 cascade (shadowmap 2048 / 1024 / 512 the same) | 11.1-11.4 | 8.35 |
| **1 cascade + shadow distance 25** | **11.07-11.32, mostly 11.11-11.18 (~89.7/s)** | 8.34 |

The last row is what Budget90 bakes. Worn over USB it runs at about 90.

The runtime's own GPU work is small (StreamingServer copy about 0.6 ms; the HEVC encode runs on the media engine).
Pacing is a fixed grid. The cutoff is Unity's GPU frame near 8.2-8.4 ms. Why that, and not about 10.5 ms, is the
limit is not understood.

### The alternative: MSAA off

`FRAMELAB_STEPS="0:msaa=1"` over the Budget90 scene, client attached (tailnet, headset unworn, one run): 0 late,
713/720 fresh, render 10.7 ms, enc+send 11.5 ms, 0 lost, 2 encoder drops; Unity GPU 7.2-7.8 ms, render thread
10.5-10.6 ms. It costs edge anti-aliasing (jaggies, and the edge shimmer returns) and showed display age 18.3 ms against 10.8
with MSAA 2x (unexplained, one run each). To keep the template's bloom and tonemap look instead, at the cost of
shimmer: `FRAMELAB_STEPS="0:msaa=1;0:postfx=1"`.

## Verified

- **Both eyes render correctly in SPI.** The client's decoded frame (`FRAME_CLIENT_DUMP=1`, 2752x1216 SBS with
  foveated encoding) shows the scene in both halves from horizontally offset viewpoints, neither blank nor a copy;
  half means 125.7 / 125.3, stdev 27.4 / 27.9. Inspected by eye as well as counted. Command:
  `FRAME_STREAM_MAC_IP=<mac-ip> FRAME_STREAM_UNITY_PROJECT=$HOME/src/unity-urp REGRESS_SECONDS=60 tools/regress.sh unity`
- No shader, Terrain/Lit, SSAO, XR or Metal error at play. URP accepts the OpenXR settings in both MultiPass and SPI.
